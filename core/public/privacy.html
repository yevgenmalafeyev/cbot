cbot

cbot is a private assistant operated by its owner for the owner's own use. It is not a public service and has no other users.

Privacy

When the owner connects a Google account, cbot reads, sends and organises mail in that account only as the owner instructs. Mail content is processed on the owner's own server and is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Access tokens are stored encrypted on that server. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
